Your measurements card

Ten short lines about your business. Every prompt in our guides starts with ‘Use my measurements card below.’ Fill it in once, keep it in your phone's notes, and paste it in after the prompt.

This page is a template to copy, not a form. Fill it in wherever you keep notes. Leave out anything private: no client or patient details.

The card

Business name:
Trade and town:
Who you serve:
Opening hours:
Price list (or a link to it):
Cancellation or deposit policy:
My tone in 3 words:
How I sign off:
Words I never use:
One thing I'm proud of:

To copy it yourself, select the text in the box and copy it.

An example, filled in

This one is for a made-up salon, to show the kind of detail that helps.

Business name: Maple Hair (a made-up salon)
Trade and town: Hair salon in Leeds
Who you serve: Busy parents and students who want low-fuss cuts and colour
Opening hours: Tue to Sat, 9am to 6pm; late night Thursday to 8pm
Price list (or a link to it): maplehair.example/prices
Cancellation or deposit policy: 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move, please
My tone in 3 words: warm, plain, unfussy
How I sign off: See you soon, Jo
Words I never use: amazing, stunning, babe
One thing I'm proud of: I show every client how to style their hair at home

Tips

  • Keep each line short. A few words is enough.
  • Write your tone and sign-off the way you'd say them, not how you think they should sound.
  • Update the card when your prices, hours or policy change.
