What it will cover

Here's a first look at the trades and roles we're starting with, and the kind of everyday jobs AI tools can help with. None of these guides are live yet.

  • Trades Coming soon

    Plumbers, electricians, builders, decorators

    • Turn voice notes from a site visit into a written quote
    • Text back missed calls with a link to book a visit
    • Ask happy customers for a review once a job is signed off

  • Salons Coming soon

    Hair, beauty, nails and barbers

    • Online booking with automatic appointment reminders
    • Draft social posts from photos of your work
    • Answer common booking and aftercare questions out of hours

  • Accountants Coming soon

    Accountants and bookkeepers

    • Read receipts and invoices into your bookkeeping software
    • Chase clients for missing documents before a deadline
    • Draft plain-English emails explaining what a client needs to do

  • Cafés Coming soon

    Cafés, restaurants and takeaways

    • Plan stock orders from what sold in past weeks
    • Draft replies to online reviews
    • Write menu descriptions and specials for the board

  • Letting agents Coming soon

    Lettings and property management

    • Draft property listings from your photos and notes
    • Log tenant repair requests and sort them by urgency
    • Turn inspection notes into a tidy written report

  • Solicitors Coming soon

    Small and high-street law firms

    • Summarise long documents for a first read, checked by a solicitor
    • Sort new enquiries by type before the first call
    • Draft routine client update letters for review

  • Florists Coming soon

    Independent florists and flower shops

    • Write descriptions for seasonal arrangements on your website
    • Plan orders around busy dates such as Valentine's Day and Mother's Day
    • Suggest card messages when a customer is stuck for words

  • Physios Coming soon

    Physiotherapists and small clinics

    • Draft session notes from a recording, for the clinician to check
    • Handle booking and rescheduling requests
    • Send patients reminders about their home exercises

How it will work

  1. Pick your trade or roleStart from the work you actually do, not from a list of tools.
  2. See what's worth tryingEach guide will explain, in plain English, what a tool does and what it's good for.
  3. Keep a person in chargeWe'll be clear about where a tool still needs your judgement and checking.
