Free guide for cafés

Reply to your Google reviews in your own voice with ChatGPT Free

  • For: Cafés and coffee shops, any team size
  • Checked on: 20 Oct 2026
  • Next check due: 18 Jan 2027

Steps written from Google's and OpenAI's help pages on 20 Oct 2026; we'll walk them through in a live account and add screenshots.

What you'll have at the end

A draft reply to each new Google review, written in your voice, that you check and post yourself.

Time needed

About 30 minutes to set up, then a few minutes for each review.

Cost

£0. ChatGPT has a free plan, and a Google Business Profile is free. Checked on 20 Oct 2026 (source 5, source 6).

Free tier: Yes, both.

Before you start

  • A verified Google Business Profile for your café. You can only reply to reviews once your business is verified.
  • The Google account that manages your Business Profile.
  • A free ChatGPT account.
  • Your measurements card.

Your measurements card

Fill in the measurements card once: ten short lines about your business, such as your opening hours, your tone and how you sign off. Every prompt below starts with it, so the drafts sound like you.

Steps

  1. In ChatGPT, turn off model training if you'd rather your chats weren't used to improve OpenAI's models.

    Where: Your profile > Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone > switch off

    You'll know it worked when the switch shows as off.

    From source 3, source 4

  2. Go to your Business Profile.

    Where: Search Google or Google Maps for your café's name, signed in to the Google account that manages it

    From source 1

  3. Open your reviews.

    Where: Business Profile > Read reviews

    From source 1

  4. Copy the words of one review. Leave out the reviewer's name.

    Where: Select the review text and copy it

    From source 2

  5. Start a new chat in ChatGPT and paste in prompt 1 below (or prompt 2 for an unhappy review), then your measurements card, then the review.

    Where: ChatGPT > New chat

  6. Read the draft and change anything that isn't true or doesn't sound like you.

    Where: Copy the draft into your notes app, or edit it in the reply box later

  7. Keep the reply short and polite, never share anything private about the reviewer, and leave out offers or promotions.

    From source 2

  8. Back on your Business Profile, find the review and select Reply.

    Where: Read reviews > Reply, next to the review

    From source 1

  9. Paste your reply and select Reply.

    Where: Reply box > Reply

    You'll know it worked when your reply shows under the review. Google checks replies first, which usually takes up to 10 minutes but can take up to 30 days. The customer is told you've replied.

    From source 1

  10. To change or remove a reply later, find it and select Edit or Delete.

    Where: Read reviews > your reply > Edit or Delete

    From source 1

  11. Pick a regular time each week to go through new reviews the same way.

    Where: Your diary

If you'd rather a chat wasn't kept in your ChatGPT history, use a temporary chat. OpenAI says temporary chats aren't used to train its models and are kept for up to 30 days. (source 3)

Ready-to-paste prompts

Paste these into ChatGPT with your measurements card and the review. Never paste the reviewer's name or anything private about them. Check every reply before you post it.

  1. 1. A happy review

    Use my measurements card below. Write a reply to this Google review of my café. Thank them in my tone, mention one specific thing they liked, keep it under 60 words, don't use any of the words I never use, and sign off the way I do. Don't add any offers.
[paste your measurements card]
Review: [paste the review, without the reviewer's name]

  2. 2. An unhappy review

    Use my measurements card below. Write a calm reply to this unhappy Google review of my café. Thank them for telling us, apologise for what went wrong without arguing, and invite them to get in touch with us directly. Keep it under 80 words, don't repeat any personal details, don't add any offers, and sign off the way I do.
[paste your measurements card]
Review: [paste the review, without the reviewer's name]

  3. 3. A mixed review

    Use my measurements card below. Write a reply to this mixed Google review of my café. Thank them for what they enjoyed, briefly acknowledge what disappointed them, and keep it under 70 words, in my tone, signed off the way I do. Don't add any offers.
[paste your measurements card]
Review: [paste the review, without the reviewer's name]

  4. 4. Short thank-yous

    Use my measurements card below. Write five different thank-you replies of under 25 words each that I can adapt for short positive reviews, so my replies don't all read the same. Use my tone and sign-off.
[paste your measurements card]

Common snags

  • If you can't see Reply, check your Business Profile is verified and that you're signed in to the Google account that manages it. (source 1)
  • If your reply doesn't show yet, Google may still be checking it. That usually takes up to 10 minutes but can take up to 30 days. If it isn't approved, you'll be asked to edit it. (source 1)
  • If ChatGPT adds something that didn't happen, such as a dish, a name or a promise, delete it before you post.
  • If a review breaks Google's rules, for example it's spam or offensive, report it instead of replying: Read reviews > Report > choose a reason > Send report. Don't report a review just because you disagree with it. (source 7)
