Free guide for physios

Draft session notes with Heidi's free plan, with patient consent

  • For: Physios and sports therapists, any team size
  • Checked on: 20 Oct 2026
  • Next check due: 18 Jan 2027

Steps written from Heidi Health's help pages on 20 Oct 2026; we'll walk them through in a live account and add screenshots.

What you'll have at the end

A draft note from each session, written by Heidi with the patient's consent, that you check before it goes into your patient records.

Time needed

About 45 minutes to set up and run a practice session, with a colleague or friend playing the patient using made-up details.

Cost

£0 on Heidi's free plan. Some features are limited on the free plan; Heidi's pricing page lists them. Checked on 20 Oct 2026 (source 5).

Free tier: Yes.

Before you start

  • A free Heidi account, from Heidi's website.
  • A laptop or phone with a working microphone and a steady internet connection.
  • The headings you usually use for notes, so you can pick the closest Heidi template.
  • The checks in 'Clinical and data protection' below, done before you use Heidi with a patient.

Clinical and data protection

  • Get the patient's consent before you use an AI scribe in a session.
  • Check every note yourself before it goes into the record. HCPC record-keeping standards still apply.
  • Check Heidi's UK GDPR terms and complete a DPIA (data protection impact assessment) before you use it with patients.
  • Never put patient-identifiable data into general chatbots such as ChatGPT.
  • Any screenshots we add to this guide will use demo patients only.

Your measurements card

Fill in the measurements card once: ten short lines about your business, such as your opening hours, your tone and how you sign off. Every prompt below starts with it, so the drafts sound like you.

Steps

  1. Check your microphone and internet connection before you start.

    Where: Your laptop or phone's built-in microphone, or an external one; wifi or mobile data

    From source 2

  2. Open Scribe and start a practice session with made-up patient details.

    Where: Left-hand menu > Scribe > add patient details

    From source 1, source 2

  3. Choose the template closest to how you write notes.

    Where: Session window > Templates

    You'll know it worked when your chosen template shows in the session.

    From source 2

  4. Add anything you want Heidi to read alongside the session, such as a referral letter. For practice, use made-up documents only.

    Where: Context tab

    From source 1, source 2

  5. Check the green waveform is showing next to the transcribe button.

    Where: Next to Start Transcribing

    You'll know it worked when the waveform moves when you speak.

    From source 1

  6. Ask the patient for consent to use an AI scribe before you start. If your clinic uses a Heidi team account, an administrator can switch on a consent pop-up that appears at the start of each session.

    Where: Before you select Start Transcribing

    From source 1, source 3, source 4

  7. Start the session.

    Where: Start Transcribing (the green button)

    You'll know it worked when the waveform and timer show it's transcribing.

    From source 1

  8. Say your findings out loud as you go. Heidi can only write up what it hears.

    Where: During the session

    From source 1

  9. Finish transcribing when the session ends.

    Where: Session window > finish transcribing

    You'll know it worked when the draft note appears in the Note tab, in your chosen template.

    From source 1, source 2

  10. Read the whole note against what happened, and correct anything wrong or missing.

    Where: Note tab; the Transcript tab shows exactly what was captured

    From source 1

  11. If the note is too long or too short, change the detail level.

    Where: Scribe settings > detail level (Brief, Goldilocks, Detailed or Super Detailed)

    From source 1

  12. Copy the checked note into the system you keep patient records in.

    Where: Copy the note, then paste it into WriteUpp, Cliniko or whichever system you use

    From source 1

  13. Run two or three more practice sessions before you use Heidi with a patient.

    Where: Scribe > new session

    From source 1

Common snags

  • If there's no green waveform, check your microphone is connected and allowed in your browser, then test it again. (source 1, source 2)
  • If the note misses something you did, you probably didn't say it out loud. Add it yourself, or use dictation to speak it into the note. (source 1)
  • If the note's layout doesn't suit you, switch templates and regenerate the note. (source 1)
  • If your internet connection drops, the Heidi mobile app can run sessions offline. (source 2)
  • If the patient says no, don't start transcribing. Write your notes the way you usually do. (source 4)
