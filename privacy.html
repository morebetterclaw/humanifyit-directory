Privacy note

Short version: we only collect what you choose to send us, we use it to tell you about Humanify It, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • If you join the list: your email address.
  • If you register a tool: your name, your email address, your tool's name and its website.

We don't ask for anything else, and this site doesn't set cookies.

Why we collect it

  • Your email address is used to send you updates about Humanify It, including when the first trade guides are ready.
  • Tool details are used to contact you about listing your tool when listings open.

We rely on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Where it goes

  • Email list: when you join, you finish signing up on the page for our newsletter, Sutra and Signal, which is run on beehiiv. beehiiv stores your email address for us and sends our emails. That page is beehiiv's, and their privacy policy applies to it. We record that you came from this site so we know the sign-up came from Humanify It.
  • Tool registrations: the form is processed by FormSubmit, which emails your details to our team inbox. FormSubmit may show a spam check (reCAPTCHA) when you send the form.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or trade your details, and we don't share them with anyone except the services named above, which handle them on our behalf.

Visitor numbers

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. It tells us things like how many people visited and which pages they looked at, without identifying you.

Unsubscribing and deletion

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
  • To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, email morebetterstudios@agentmail.to and we'll deal with it.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
